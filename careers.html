What we expect

The standard starts before your first shift.

Blackstar operates in environments where reliability, communication and judgement matter. Our recruitment process is designed to make sure applicants understand what is required before they apply.

If something is missing, we would rather show you what needs to be fixed than simply tell you that you cannot continue.

01

Prepared

Your licence, right-to-work evidence, identity documents and recruitment information should be ready when requested.

02

Reliable

Security teams depend on people who arrive on time, communicate early and can be trusted to attend the shifts they accept.

03

Professional

You may be the first person a customer, member of staff or visitor meets. How you communicate matters.

04

Accountable

Reports, instructions and site procedures need to be completed accurately and followed consistently.

Application readiness

Are you ready to apply?

This assessment checks the practical requirements that commonly delay or prevent security applications from progressing.

It is guidance, not an employment decision. Some circumstances need to be reviewed by a member of the recruitment team.

Blackstar readiness check Role & Availability Question 1 of 20
01 Which Blackstar role are you interested in?

Choose the role that best matches what you want to apply for.

02 Can you currently prove that you have the legal right to work in the UK?
03 How would you prove your right to work?
04 Are the personal details on your identity and right-to-work documents accurate and consistent?

For example, your name and date of birth should be accurate. If you have legitimately changed your name, supporting evidence may be required during recruitment.

05 Do you currently hold an SIA licence?
06 Which SIA licence do you hold or are you applying for?
07 What is the current status of your SIA licence?
08 Do you hold the current training or refresher qualification required for your licence?
09 During the last 10 years, have you spent 6 continuous months or more outside the UK?
10 If overseas criminal-record evidence is required, could you provide it?
11 Can you provide a complete 5-year address, employment and education history?

Genuine periods when you were not working or studying can still be recorded. The important thing is that the timeline is accurate and complete.

12 Can you provide suitable referees who can verify your employment or history?
13 Are you aware of any criminal record, investigation or other matter that could affect your SIA licence or suitability for security work?

Do not enter conviction details here. This question only determines whether a confidential recruitment review may be needed.

14 A site instruction says: “All visitors must sign in at reception and display a visitor pass.” A visitor says they will only be five minutes and does not want to sign in. What should you do?
15 Which is the best example of an incident report entry?
16 Your supervisor gives you an instruction that you do not fully understand. What would you do?
17 Can you reliably travel to and from assigned sites, including after late-night shifts?
18 Which shifts can you reliably commit to?
19 Your shift begins at 19:00. When would you normally aim to arrive?
20 Recruitment asks you to provide an updated document by Friday at 17:00. What would you normally do?
