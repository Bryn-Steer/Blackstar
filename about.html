01Our philosophy

Security should improve the place it protects.

  1. What we believe
  2. How we approach it
  3. Why people come first

Blackstar Security is a Manchester security company, established in 2013 and operating as Blackstar Group Solutions Ltd. We provide door supervision, event security, safety stewarding, security guarding, school security, and residential and concierge security.

01

What we believe

Good security should never make a great venue feel less welcoming. Customers should feel at ease, staff should feel supported and the people responsible for a site should feel in control.

02

How we approach it

Every environment works differently, so we start with the place rather than a headcount. We learn how your venue, event or site operates and where the pressure points are, then decide what the team needs to look like.

03

Why people come first

Security only works when the person matches the assignment. Professionalism, judgement and accountability are what customers notice, long before anything goes wrong.

03The people behind Blackstar

The right people change how security feels.

Professional security depends on the person in the role. We select people for the way they carry themselves as much as for their licence: professional enough to represent your business, approachable enough to support your customers and prepared enough to act when something changes.

Careers at Blackstar

  • Judgement.

    Knowing when to step in, when to step back and how to calm a situation before it escalates.

  • Communication.

    Clear, courteous and approachable with guests, staff and the people we report to.

  • Character.

    Reliable, discreet and accountable, on every shift and in every environment.
