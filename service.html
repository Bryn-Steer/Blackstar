Door supervision

Firm when needed, welcoming at every other moment.

What we provide
Professional SIA licensed door supervisors who manage entry, set the tone at the door and are ready to act when the situation demands it.
Where it fits
Nightclubs, bars, hotels, live music venues and licensed events, for one-off, short-notice and ongoing requirements.
The problem it solves
The door is the first impression. Handled badly, it creates friction; handled well, customers feel welcome and the venue stays in control.
  • A stronger first impression
  • Customers feel welcome
  • Confidence when it matters

Event security

Protecting the event without becoming the event.

What we provide
Security for entry points, restricted areas, crowd management and incident response, planned around how your event runs.
Where it fits
Concerts, festivals, sporting fixtures, conferences, live entertainment and public events.
The problem it solves
Busy events need controlled access and a quick response, without slowing guests down or changing the atmosphere they came for.
  • A smoother guest experience
  • Crowds kept under control
  • The event stays the focus
Event security in detail

03 / 06

Safety stewarding

Clear guidance that helps people move safely and confidently.

What we provide
Approachable stewards who guide guests, manage crowd flow and support organisers and the wider security operation.
Where it fits
Stadiums, sporting fixtures, concerts, festivals, conferences and public events.
Stewarding or security?
Genuine stewarding duties can fall outside SIA licensing. Where duties amount to licensable security activity, appropriately licensed personnel are required, and requirements involving both can be discussed.
  • Clear guidance throughout
  • People move with confidence
  • A safer, calmer environment

Manned guarding

Visible, accountable protection for premises and sites.

What we provide
Security guards for access control, patrols and site protection, giving your premises a visible presence and an immediate human response.
Where it fits
Commercial premises, industrial sites, managed property and businesses.
The problem it solves
Sites need someone present, accountable and able to respond, so issues are handled professionally rather than discovered later.
  • A reassuring visible presence
  • Your site stays protected
  • Issues handled professionally

School security

Safeguarding-focused security that never feels intimidating.

What we provide
Support with access control, visitor procedures and site security, with clearly defined security responsibilities.
Where it fits
Schools, colleges and education settings.
How it works with safeguarding
Security complements the school’s own safeguarding and security arrangements. Safeguarding responsibilities remain with the school and its designated personnel.
  • A safer school environment
  • Approachable around pupils
  • Support staff can rely on

Residential buildings need to feel welcoming and secure at the same time, for residents, visitors and the people who manage the property.
