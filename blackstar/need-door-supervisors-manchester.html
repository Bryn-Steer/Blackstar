Do I Need Door Supervisors for My Bar, Pub or Nightclub in Manchester?

When Manchester venues need SIA licensed door supervisors, what your premises licence may require and how to decide the right door-security team for your business.

Manchester Door Security

The Short Answer

Not every bar, pub or nightclub in Manchester automatically has to employ door supervisors simply because it is licensed to sell alcohol.

Whether your venue must have door supervisors can depend on the conditions attached to its individual premises licence. Those conditions may specify when SIA registered door supervisors must work, how many are required and where they need to be positioned.

There is also a separate SIA licensing question. If somebody is guarding licensed premises against damage, theft, unauthorised access or disorderly behaviour, that is door-supervision activity and the operative needs the appropriate SIA Door Supervisor licence.

So if you run a Manchester bar, pub, nightclub or other licensed venue, the safest starting point is: check your premises licence, understand the security activities being performed and build the door team around your actual risks and operating conditions.

Discuss Your Venue
Know the Requirement

Are Door Supervisors a Legal Requirement for Pubs, Bars and Nightclubs?

There is no blanket rule saying every pub, bar or nightclub in the UK must employ a fixed number of door supervisors.

The important document is your premises licence. Conditions attached to that licence set the parameters within which the premises can lawfully operate.

If your premises licence says that SIA registered door supervisors must be on duty during particular days, times or events, those conditions need to be followed.

Important

Selling Alcohol Does Not Create One Automatic Door-Supervisor Rule

Two Manchester venues can both sell alcohol but have different door-supervision requirements because their premises licences, size, location, operating hours, customers and risk profiles may be different.

SIA Licensing

When Does Someone Need an SIA Door Supervisor Licence?

The Security Industry Authority states that a Door Supervisor licence is required when somebody guards licensed premises against:

  • Damage
  • Theft
  • Unauthorised access
  • Disorderly behaviour

For SIA purposes, licensed premises include venues licensed to sell alcohol for consumption on the premises or provide relevant regulated entertainment.

Importantly, door supervision at licensed premises is one of the security activities for which an SIA licence can still be required even when the person works directly for the venue rather than through an external security company.

Manchester Premises Licences

What Can a Manchester Premises Licence Require?

Manchester City Council publishes model licensing conditions showing the types of door-supervision requirements that can be incorporated into an individual premises licence.

These model conditions are not automatically imposed on every venue. Your actual premises licence is what matters.

Depending on the licence, conditions can cover:

  • Which days or circumstances door supervision is required
  • What time door supervisors must start and finish
  • A specified minimum number of SIA registered door supervisors
  • A specified door-supervisor-to-customer ratio
  • Where door supervisors must be positioned
  • Whether a specified number of female door supervisors is required
  • Identification, clothing or visibility requirements
  • Body-worn video requirements
  • Whether door supervisors must be supplied by an SIA Approved Contractor
Staffing Levels

How Many Door Supervisors Do I Need in Manchester?

There is no universal Manchester rule saying every venue needs one door supervisor for a fixed number of customers.

Your premises licence may specify an exact minimum number or ratio. If it does, that condition should form the starting point for your security deployment.

Where staffing is based on a risk assessment, Manchester City Council's model conditions identify several factors that may influence the number of door supervisors a venue needs.

Factors to Consider

  • Size of the venue
  • Expected attendance
  • Type of event or trading night
  • Location of the premises
  • Time of year
  • Special occasions such as New Year's Eve or major local events
  • Conditions already attached to the premises licence
Beyond the Minimum

When Should a Venue Consider Door Supervisors Even If More Are Not Specifically Required?

Licence conditions establish what your venue is required to do. Operational risk can still justify additional door security in Manchester on particular nights.

Busy Friday and Saturday Nights

Higher footfall can put more pressure on queues, entrances, customer movement and venue staff. Extra coverage may help the venue remain controlled without putting one person in several places at once.

Ticketed or Promoted Events

A special event can change the normal crowd, arrival pattern and risk profile of a venue. Security staffing should reflect what is actually happening that night.

Football, Concert and Major City-Centre Nights

Major events elsewhere in Manchester can affect customer numbers, arrival times and the atmosphere around licensed premises. A venue's security plan may need to adjust accordingly.

Queues and Multiple Entrances

Venues with several access points, smoking areas or substantial external queues can need more security positions than a simple capacity calculation suggests.

Previous Disorder or Recurring Problems

If particular nights, areas or customer flows have repeatedly caused problems, those patterns should feed into the venue's risk assessment and security planning.

When Staff Need Reliable Support

Door supervision is not only about the entrance. A properly planned security team can give managers and venue staff confidence that trained people are available when a security situation develops.

More Than the Door

What Do Door Supervisors Do at a Manchester Venue?

Professional SIA door supervisors in Manchester can perform a wider security role than simply standing outside the entrance.

  • Support controlled entry and protect against unauthorised access
  • Manage queues and busy entrance areas
  • Monitor behaviour and recognise developing problems
  • Support agreed venue entry policies
  • Respond appropriately to disorder or security incidents
  • Help manage safe customer dispersal at closing time
  • Communicate with venue management and other members of the security team
  • Help protect customers, staff and the premises
Entry Procedures

Do Door Supervisors Check ID and Search Customers?

Door supervisors can be involved in ID checks and agreed search procedures at licensed premises, depending on the venue's policies and licence conditions.

Manchester's model licensing conditions include examples where people entering a premises must be searched by an SIA registered door supervisor and where identification or entry-control systems form part of the premises conditions.

Again, those are examples of conditions that may be applied. Venue owners and managers should follow the conditions and procedures that actually apply to their premises.

The Right Licence

Can I Use Security Guards Instead of Door Supervisors?

Not where the role is licensable door supervision at licensed premises. A Security Guard licence does not provide the same licence scope as a Door Supervisor licence.

An appropriately licensed door supervisor can also perform licensable security-guarding activity, but somebody holding only a Security Guard licence cannot simply perform door-supervision work that requires a Door Supervisor licence.

Professional Door Security

Are Door Supervisors the Same as Bouncers or Doormen?

People still commonly use terms such as doormen, door staff or bouncers when talking about nightclub and bar security.

The regulated professional role is door supervisor. It involves licensed security responsibilities and should be treated as more than simply placing somebody at the entrance. For Blackstar, the job is to protect the venue while also representing the business properly in front of its customers.

Your Front Door Matters

Door Security Should Protect Your Venue Without Damaging the Experience

Your security team may be the first people customers meet when they arrive and the last people they see when they leave.

That makes good bar and nightclub security in Manchester about more than responding when something goes wrong. Presentation, communication, judgement and the way customers are welcomed all influence how the venue feels.

The right door team protects the environment in the background, gives staff confidence and knows when a situation genuinely requires intervention.

Hiring Door Staff

How Do I Choose Door Supervisors in Manchester?

If you're comparing door supervision companies in Manchester, do not assess them purely by the cheapest hourly rate.

Ask the security provider:

  • How do you verify every operative's SIA Door Supervisor licence?
  • Are you an SIA Approved Contractor for Door Supervision?
  • How will you understand our premises licence and venue procedures?
  • Who manages the team once the contract starts?
  • What happens when someone is absent at short notice?
  • How do you brief security staff on our customers and venue?
  • How are incidents communicated and reported to management?
