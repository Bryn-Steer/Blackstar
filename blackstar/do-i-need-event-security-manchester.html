Do I Need Security for My Event in Manchester?

When event security is required, when it is strongly worth considering and how to choose the right security guards, door supervisors and stewards for an event in Manchester.

Manchester Event Security

The Short Answer

Not every event in Manchester automatically needs a team of professional security guards. Whether you need event security in Manchester depends on the venue, licensing arrangements, number of guests, alcohol, type of event, access points, expected risks and the duties you need security personnel to perform.

However, if your event involves large crowds, alcohol, controlled entry, valuable equipment, restricted areas, high-profile guests, previous incidents or a significant risk of disorder, professional event security guards may form an important part of your event plan.

The important distinction is between security and stewarding. Someone directing guests to seats or facilities may be performing a stewarding role, while controlling access, guarding against disorder or protecting licensed premises can become licensable security activity requiring appropriately licensed personnel.

Licensing & Security

When Does an Event Need SIA Licensed Security?

The requirement for an SIA licence is based on what the person is actually doing, not simply whether their job title says "security" or "steward".

Events at Licensed Premises

If an operative provides a physical presence to guard licensed premises against damage, theft, unauthorised access or disorderly behaviour while alcohol is being supplied for consumption or regulated entertainment is taking place, the role can require an SIA Door Supervisor licence.

This is particularly relevant to event security in Manchester for licensed venues, bars, music events, entertainment venues and other events where alcohol or regulated entertainment forms part of the operation.

Events at Other Premises

At non-licensed premises, contracted staff providing guarding against theft, damage, unauthorised access or disorder may require the appropriate SIA Security Guard licence. The correct licence depends on the activity being performed.

Your Event Licence or Venue May Also Set Requirements

Event organisers should also check the conditions and requirements applying to their particular venue or licensed premises. In Manchester, licensing conditions can specify when door supervisors must be present, how many are required and where they are deployed.

Assess the Event

When Should I Hire Event Security in Manchester?

Even where security is not automatically required by law, there are situations where hiring professional event security in Manchester should be considered as part of your risk planning.

1. Your Event Will Attract a Large Crowd

As attendance increases, so does the need to think about entrances, exits, queues, crowd movement, restricted areas and how incidents would be managed. Professional event security guards can provide clear roles around these pressure points.

2. Alcohol Will Be Served

Alcohol can materially change the security profile of an event. Where the security activity relates to licensed premises, appropriately licensed SIA door supervisors may be required for relevant guarding duties.

3. You Need Controlled Entry

If your event needs security personnel to prevent unauthorised entry, enforce restricted areas or remove people where necessary, you need to understand whether those duties are licensable. This is different from a steward simply checking a ticket or directing somebody to the correct entrance.

4. There Are Valuable Assets or Restricted Areas

Events can involve audiovisual equipment, merchandise, vehicles, backstage areas, stock, cash or other assets. Professional security can help control access and protect areas that should not be open to the general public.

5. The Event Has High-Profile Guests or Performers

Performers, speakers, executives and other high-profile attendees can introduce additional access-control and security considerations. The appropriate security response should be assessed around the actual risks involved.

6. You Expect Queues or Busy Entry Points

Entry is one of the first places an event can begin to feel disorganised. Well-planned Manchester event security can help protect access points while supporting a smoother arrival experience for guests.

7. Your Risk Assessment Identifies a Need

The correct level of security should ultimately reflect the actual event. Attendance, venue layout, event type, location, alcohol, operating hours and known risks should all influence the security plan rather than relying on a generic formula.

Know the Difference

Event Security vs Event Stewards: What Is the Difference?

This is one of the most important distinctions for anyone organising an event.

Event Stewards

Pure stewarding duties can include showing people to seats, giving directions and helping guests find toilets, first-aid facilities or other parts of the venue. Those duties alone do not necessarily require an SIA licence.

Event Security Officers

Security work can include guarding against unauthorised access, damage, theft or disorder and carrying out other licensable security activities. Where the activity is licensable, the operative must hold the appropriate SIA security licence.

Event Protection

What Does Event Security in Manchester Actually Do?

The role of an event security company in Manchester should be shaped around the event, rather than treating every assignment in exactly the same way.

  • Protect entrances and help prevent unauthorised access
  • Support safe and organised queue management
  • Protect restricted, backstage or staff-only areas
  • Monitor behaviour and identify developing problems
  • Respond appropriately to disorder or security incidents
  • Protect staff, visitors, property and equipment
  • Support crowd movement around key areas of the event
  • Work alongside event organisers and other responsible teams when an incident requires a coordinated response
Different Events. Different Risks.

What Types of Events May Need Security in Manchester?

Blackstar can provide professional security for events in Manchester across a range of environments. The exact combination of security officers, door supervisors and stewards should depend on the event itself.

Festivals & Outdoor Events

Outdoor events can create challenges around perimeter control, multiple entrances, crowd movement, restricted areas and protecting temporary infrastructure.

Concerts & Live Entertainment

Live entertainment can require controlled entry, queue management, backstage protection and appropriately licensed personnel where regulated security activities are being carried out.

Corporate Events

Corporate event security in Manchester may focus on professional access control, guest management, restricted areas and protecting the environment without disrupting the event experience.

Sporting Events

Sporting environments may require a combination of stewarding, crowd management and licensable security duties depending on the ground, event and contractual arrangements.

Private & Ticketed Events

Private celebrations and ticketed events may benefit from controlled entry, guest-list management and security support where the scale, venue or risk profile makes it appropriate.

Event Staffing

How Many Security Guards Do I Need for My Event?

There is no single UK security-guard-to-guest ratio that applies automatically to every event. The correct number should reflect your risk assessment, venue requirements, licensing conditions and the way the event operates.

Manchester licensing conditions can, where applied to a premises, specify numbers or ratios of SIA registered door supervisors. Factors identified by Manchester City Council include venue size, expected attendance, event type, location, time of year, special occasions and premises licence conditions.

Your security plan may therefore need people covering different positions rather than simply multiplying total attendance by a generic number.

Preparing for Change

What Does Martyn's Law Mean for Events in Manchester?

The Terrorism (Protection of Premises) Act 2025, commonly known as Martyn's Law, introduces new protective-security requirements for certain publicly accessible premises and events.

As of August 2026, the legislation has not yet come into force. The Security Industry Authority currently expects commencement in spring 2027.

Martyn's Law does not simply mean that every affected event must hire a certain number of security guards. Responsibility remains with the person or organisation responsible for the qualifying premises or event, although suitably knowledgeable security professionals can be used for advice and support.

Choosing Event Security

How Do I Choose an Event Security Company in Manchester?

If you are comparing event security companies in Manchester, look beyond how many guards a provider says it can supply. You need to understand whether the people being deployed are appropriate for the actual roles they will perform and how the team will be managed throughout the event.

  • Check the appropriate SIA licences for licensable roles
  • Ask who will manage and supervise the security deployment
  • Make sure the provider understands your venue and event plan
  • Clarify which roles are security and which are stewarding
  • Ask how incidents and operational changes will be communicated
  • Choose a team that can protect the event without becoming the event
